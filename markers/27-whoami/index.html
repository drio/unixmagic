whoami

Prints the effective user of the current shell

whoami prints the name of the user the current process runs as. Bill Joy added it in 2.9BSD as a short form of who am i, but today the two answer different questions, and the difference shows up right after su.

who am i looks your terminal up in the login records, so it reports who logged in. whoami asks the kernel for the process’s effective user ID. After su, they disagree:

$ su
# who am i
alice    pts/0    ...
# whoami
root
-- EOF --
