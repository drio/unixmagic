The tree the wizard is working on probably stands for the Unix filesystem
hierarchy. Unix organizes files and directories as a tree rooted at `/`,
branching into subdirectories like `/usr`, `/bin`, and `/etc`. You navigate it with commands like `cd`, `ls`, and `pwd`.

The branching form could also represent process trees. Every process in Unix
has a parent, forming a tree rooted at `init` (PID 1).
